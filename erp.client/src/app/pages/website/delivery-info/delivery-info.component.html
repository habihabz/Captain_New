Delivery Information

Learn about our shipping and delivery processes

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Shipping Policies

We strive to deliver your orders as quickly and efficiently as possible. Our standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. We partner with reliable courier services to ensure your products reach you safely and on time.

2. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges may vary based on your location, the total weight of the items, and the chosen delivery method. You can view the exact shipping cost during the checkout process before completing your purchase. We occasionally offer free shipping promotions, which will be prominently displayed on our website.

3. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS to track your delivery status. You can also monitor the progress of your delivery by logging into your account and navigating to the 'My Orders' section.

4. Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with your delivery, such as a delayed shipment or receiving a damaged package, please contact our customer support team immediately. We will investigate the matter and work towards a swift resolution.
