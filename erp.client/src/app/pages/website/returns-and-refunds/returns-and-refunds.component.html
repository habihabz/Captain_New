Returns & Refunds

Everything you need to know about our return policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Return Policy

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. The items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all tags intact. We reserve the right to reject returns that do not meet these conditions.

2. Refund Process

Once we receive your returned item, our team will inspect it and process your refund within 3-5 business days. The amount will be credited back to your original method of payment. Please note that shipping charges are non-refundable.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain items like perishable goods, intimate apparel, customized or personalized products, and items marked as 'final sale' cannot be returned. Please review the product description carefully before making a purchase.

4. Exchanges

If you need to exchange an item for a different size or color, please follow the standard return process for the original item and place a new order for the desired replacement.
