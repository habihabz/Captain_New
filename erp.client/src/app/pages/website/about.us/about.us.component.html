About Captain

ABOUT

Founded in 2012 in Kerala, India, Captain is a fast-growing multinational sports and apparel brand dedicated to performance, innovation, and accessibility. Driven by the belief that sport builds character and opportunity, Captain develops reliable, performance-focused equipment for athletes at every level. More than products, Captain stands for a vision — empowering future talents and inspiring the next generation of players.

Vision & Mission

VISION

To become a globally recognized sports brand that empowers athletes of all levels to achieve their fullest potential through innovative, durable, and stylish performance gear.

MISSION

To equip every athlete with reliable gear that fuels confidence, ambition, and progress.

Milestone

MILESTONE

Starting with revolutionary football socks in 2012, Captain now stands strong with 120+
sports essentials, built with the spirit and pride of athletes.

Innovation

INNOVATION

At Captain, innovation goes beyond technology — it begins with understanding athletes and creating smarter, stronger gear. Every product reflects a commitment to continuous improvement, precision engineering, and forward-thinking design that evolves with the future of sport.
